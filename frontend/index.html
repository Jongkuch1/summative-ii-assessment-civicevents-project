Our Mission

About CivicEvents

CivicEvents is an open, accessible portal designed to bridge the gap between city government and citizens. Our mission is to make civic participation easier, more transparent, and accessible to everyone.

Open & Transparent

All published content is publicly listed. Citizens can see what's happening in their city at any time.

Built for Accessibility

Semantic HTML, ARIA labels, keyboard navigation, captions for audio/video, and responsive design throughout.

Secure by Design

Role-based permissions, token authentication, and server-side enforcement ensure your data stays safe.
